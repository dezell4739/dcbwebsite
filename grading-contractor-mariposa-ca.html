Why Proper Grading Matters in Mariposa County

Grading is critical for managing water flow, preventing erosion, and creating stable surfaces for construction. In foothill areas like Mariposa, improper grading can lead to washouts, drainage problems, driveway failure, and long-term structural issues.

A driveway, building pad, or road may look fine when it is dry, but poor slope and drainage can show up fast during heavy rain. That is why DCB focuses on practical grading solutions that consider the terrain, access, water movement, and the way the property will actually be used.
