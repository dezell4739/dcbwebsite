Frequently Asked Questions

What does land prep include?

Land prep can include clearing, grading, pad preparation, access improvements, rough site cleanup, and preparing the property for the next construction or improvement phase depending on project conditions.

Do you prepare rural and hillside properties?

Yes. DCB works on rural and mountain properties in Mariposa County and plans land prep around access, terrain, drainage, and equipment needs.

Can you prep land before a shop, home, or other structure is built?

Yes. We can prepare sites for future construction by improving access, clearing material, rough grading, and creating a better starting point for the next phase.

Do you offer cleanup and haul-off with land prep?

Yes. Depending on the project, cleanup and haul-off can be included so the property is cleaner and more usable when the work is complete.
