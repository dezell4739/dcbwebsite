Land Preparation for Rural Properties

Land prep is where many projects really begin. Before construction, trenching, grading, or utility work can happen, the site often needs clearing, access improvements, cleanup, shaping, or general preparation.

DCB provides land prep services in Mariposa County for foothill and rural properties where access, terrain, slope, and long-term usability matter. We help property owners get the site ready so the next phase can move forward without unnecessary delays.

Whether you are preparing for a future build, cleaning up an overgrown property, improving access, or getting land ready for excavation and grading, we approach the work with practical planning and clear communication.

Site Cleanup

Remove debris, overgrowth, and obstacles so the property is ready for the next phase of work.

Access Improvements

Improve site access for equipment, material delivery, and future construction activity.

Brush Clearing Support

Prepare overgrown properties for safer access, defensible space, and usable project areas.

Prep for Grading & Excavation

Get the site ready for house pads, trenches, grading work, utility routes, and construction planning.
