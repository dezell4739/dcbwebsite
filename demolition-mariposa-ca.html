Residential and Light Demolition Services

Interior Demolition

We provide interior demolition for remodel preparation, including removal of cabinets, drywall, flooring, fixtures, and non-structural materials. This is a good fit for kitchen remodels, bathroom remodels, and full interior tear-outs before rebuilding.

Structure Removal

DCB can remove sheds, decks, porches, small outbuildings, damaged structures, and other unwanted improvements depending on access and job conditions. We focus on controlled removal and a clean finish.

Debris Haul-Off

Demolition is only part of the job. We can load, haul, and clean up demolition debris so your property is not left with piles of material in the way of the next phase.

Selective Demolition

When only part of a structure needs to come out, selective demolition helps preserve surrounding areas while removing the material that needs to go. This can be useful for remodels, repairs, and phased property improvements.
